Common questions about GST and ITR filing

Short answers to what new clients ask us most. Need something specific? Ask our team.

Who needs to file GST Returns?

Businesses registered under GST file periodic returns such as GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, including Nil Returns in months with no activity.

What happens if I miss a filing deadline?

Late fees and interest may apply, and prolonged non-filing can lead to further action such as cancellation of GST registration. Filing late is usually better than not filing.

Can you reply to a GST or Income Tax notice?

Yes. We review the notice, prepare the reply with supporting documents and represent you where required.

Which documents do I need to start?

For GST: sales and purchase invoices and your GST portal access. For ITR: PAN, Form 16 or income details, AIS/26AS and investment proofs.
