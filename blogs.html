GST

GSTR-1 vs GSTR-3B: what is the difference?

Read article

GSTR-1 reports your outward supplies, while GSTR-3B is the summary return where tax is paid. The two should reconcile, since mismatches are a common reason for notices.

GST

Why you should reconcile GSTR-2B every month

Read article

GSTR-2B shows the input tax credit available to you. Comparing it with your purchase records each month helps you claim only what is eligible and spot supplier defaults early.

Old vs new tax regime: how to choose
